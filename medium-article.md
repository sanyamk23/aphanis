## 🔍 Part 1: How Zero-Width Steganography Works

Steganography is the practice of concealing a secret message within an ordinary document. In AI outputs, models encode metadata bits by substituting normal space characters with invisible zero-width Unicode characters.

For example, to a human reader, this sentence looks completely normal:
> *"The system is operating normally."*

However, when inspecting the raw UTF-8 byte stream, invisible zero-width spaces are injected between letters:
```text
T h e \u200B s y s t e m \uFEFF i s \u200C o p e r a t i n g
```

Detectors (like GPTZero, Turnitin, or Copyleaks) scan for these exact byte patterns to instantly verify if text originated from an AI.

## ✍️ Part 3: Automatic Tone Humanization

Stripping zero-width bytes is only half the battle. AI models leave distinct linguistic footprints, such as avoiding contractions (*"it is"* instead of *"it's"*) and overusing wordy passive filler phrases (*"in order to"*, *"it is important to note that"*).

Untrace AI features an **Automatic Natural Humanizer Engine**:
- **Contraction Synthesizer**: Converts rigid expansions (*"it is"* $\rightarrow$ *"it's"*, *"cannot"* $\rightarrow$ *"can't"*, *"do not"* $\rightarrow$ *"don't"*, *"we have"* $\rightarrow$ *"we've"*).
- **Passive Filler Reducer**: Replaces 50+ robotic phrases (*"in order to"* $\rightarrow$ *"to"*, *"due to the fact that"* $\rightarrow$ *"because"*).
- **Tone Personas**: Choose target human writing styles (`conversational`, `tech-lead`, `academic`, `executive`).

```python
from untrace import clean_text

cleaned = clean_text("Furthermore, it is important to note that we cannot fail.", tone="tech-lead")
print(cleaned)
# Output: "Also, note that we can't fail."
```
