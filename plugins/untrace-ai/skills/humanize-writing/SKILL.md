# Humanize Writing Skill

Use this skill whenever generating prose, articles, documentation, or responses to ensure the writing sounds 100% human and free of AI stylistic telltales.

## Core Transformation Rules

1. **Use Natural Contractions**:
   - Convert expanded forms (*it is* $\rightarrow$ *it's*, *do not* $\rightarrow$ *don't*, *cannot* $\rightarrow$ *can't*, *they are* $\rightarrow$ *they're*).

2. **Purge Robotic Filler & Clichés**:
   - Eliminate filler phrases (*in order to* $\rightarrow$ *to*, *it is important to note that* $\rightarrow$ *note that*, *due to the fact that* $\rightarrow$ *because*).
   - Never use AI vocabulary markers (*delve*, *testament*, *tapestry*, *pivotal*, *crucial*, *multifaceted*, *spearhead*).

3. **Punctuation & Rhythm**:
   - Avoid em-dashes (`—`). Use colons, commas, or standard hyphens.
   - Vary sentence lengths dramatically (mix short 3-word punches with longer descriptive lines to achieve high burstiness).
